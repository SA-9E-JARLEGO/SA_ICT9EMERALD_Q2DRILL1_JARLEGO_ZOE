ALL ABOUT BADMINTON

A racket sport played using a shuttlecock across a net.

The Basics

01 / Rules

  • Hit the shuttlecock over the net.
  • Keep it inside the court lines.
  • Games are usually played to 21 points.

02 / Equipment

  • Racket
  • Shuttlecock
  • Net
  • Sports shoes

03 / Techniques

  • Serving
  • Forehand
  • Backhand
  • Smash
